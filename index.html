Questions

Before you call: a few quick answers.

Is GoBilling desktop or online software?

GoBilling is a desktop ERP. Business data is stored locally on your computer, with licensing and optional backup services connected online.

Can GoBilling create GST invoices?

Yes. GoBilling supports GST invoices with SGST/CGST or IGST, HSN/SAC summaries, taxable values, and GST reports.

Can I manage jewellery stock in GoBilling?

Yes. You can manage tagged stock, opening stock, batch stock, item photos, weights, purity, HUID, and item availability.

Does GoBilling support karigar job work?

Yes. GoBilling includes karigar master and karigar job work tracking for items or metal given to artisans and received back.

Does GoBilling support saving schemes?

Yes. GoBilling includes monthly saving schemes, including cash schemes and gold/silver weight-based schemes.

Is backup available?

Yes. GoBilling supports local database backup, extra folder backup, and optional online backup.
